# Navigate

Photon is the telemetry page for the car. It shows decoded CAN, the onboard camera, and a 3D view of the car on track. This guide is the mechanical workflow: open a live car or a saved run, read the chassis tabs, build a plot, and take a CSV home.

Electrical detail stays on Pit and Dashboard. Mechanical is the chassis, driver, and dynamics page. Every screen shares one playhead.

## Read this first

1. [Open the page](/open.html) and sign in.
2. [Top bar](/top-bar.html) to pick a screen.
3. [Logs](/logs.html) so the charts are looking at the moment you care about.
4. [Mechanical](/mechanical/) for pedals, brakes, IMUs, suspension, yaw, wheels, and the G-G.
5. [Plots](/plots.html) when the built-in 15 second charts are the wrong shape.
6. [Export a CSV](/export.html) when the stint needs a spreadsheet.

::: tip
Charts on Mechanical always draw the last 15 seconds at the Logs playhead. Move that playhead before you decide a channel is dead.
:::
