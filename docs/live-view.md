# Live View

Live View is the camera and the 3D car, without the chassis charts. Open it when you need the picture large, then go back to Mechanical or Plots for the numbers. Both follow the [Logs](/logs.html) playhead.

On a wide window the 3D pane and the camera sit side by side. Drag the splitter between them. On a narrow or tall window the 3D pane stacks above the camera, and the splitter moves up and down.

The 3D header shows the fix. A muted GPS note means the fix is missing for this moment. The car model can still be on the pane.

The camera shows the recorded or live picture for this playhead. **BUFFERING** in LOGS means the picture is still loading. A run whose video does not overlap the CAN still has traces and an empty picture. Scrub to a clock inside the stint. If the picture stays empty and the charts are moving, that run has no camera there.

Race context on Mechanical is a small copy of these two panes. Use Live View to see the corner. Use Mechanical to read the traces for the same clock.

::: tip
Pause before you compare a frame with a chart. Play keeps both moving, and a 15 second Mechanical window will have slid by the time you look back.
:::
