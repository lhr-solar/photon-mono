# Photon for Mechanical

Photon is the car telemetry page. It shows decoded CAN from the car, the camera, and a 3D view of the car on track. This book is the mechanical workflow: open a live car or a saved run, read the chassis tabs, build a plot, and take a CSV home.

Electrical detail stays on Pit and Dashboard. Mechanical is the chassis, driver, and dynamics page, and it shares one playhead with the rest of the window.

## What to read first

1. [Open the page](open.md) and sign in.
2. [Live and replay](replay.md) so the charts are looking at the moment you care about.
3. [Tabs](tabs.md) to pick the right screen.
4. [Mechanical](mechanical/README.md) for pedals, brakes, IMUs, suspension, yaw, wheels, and the G-G.
5. [Plots](plots.md) when the built-in 15 second charts are the wrong shape.
6. [Export a CSV](export.md) when the stint needs a spreadsheet.

{% hint style="info" %}
Charts on Mechanical always draw the last 15 seconds at the Logs playhead. Move that playhead before you decide a channel is dead.
{% endhint %}
